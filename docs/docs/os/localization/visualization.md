---
title: Visualization
---

Visualization is the 3D workspace that shows the currently selected profile, field map, detections, and solved poses.

## What you can do

- Inspect tag detections and solved poses in 3D.
- Compare field-relative output against the selected field map.
- Review source/profile overlays while tuning solver configuration.
- Confirm whether calibration, field origin, and selected sources are producing the expected solve.
