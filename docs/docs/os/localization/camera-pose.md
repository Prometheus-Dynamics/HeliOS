---
title: Camera Pose
---

Camera pose is the per-camera extrinsic transform used by localization and the 3D viewers.

## What you can do

- Edit translation and rotation for stream-backed cameras.
- Clear or replace saved pose values.
- Cross-check stream pose against the rig layout / camera layout view.

In practice, most pose editing happens from the stream **Pose** tab or the rig layout tools; Localization consumes that saved pose data.
