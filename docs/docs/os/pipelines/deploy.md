---
title: Deploy and Attach
---

Attach a pipeline to a live capture stream.

## What you can do

- Select a stream.
- Assign the selected pipeline to that stream.

## Where to do it

- In the `Graph` tab: use the `Assign` action for the current pipeline.
- In the `Tune` tab: click the `+` button next to the stream tabs.

## Notes

- The assign modal only lists live streams that the device can see.
- Attaching a pipeline affects what that stream produces (outputs, detections, and any downstream consumers).
